## Overview

Start organizing your documentation easily.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Your Dashboard">
    Log in at `https://docs.denisov.com` and click on "Dashboard".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Project">
    Click "New Project", give it a name (e.g. "API Docs"), and hit "Create".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Writing">
    Open your project and click "Add Page" to create your first doc!
  </Step>
</Steps>
